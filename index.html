How a run works

Four steps, eight tables, one run.

  1. Play the hand

    Two hole cards each, five on the board. You act first: check, bet any amount, raise, fold or go all in.

  2. Charms pay out

    Win and your charms trigger left to right, each with its own effect.

  3. Shop between tables

    Buy, sell and reorder charms. Level up the hands you like winning with.

  4. Climb the casino

    Hit each table's chip target in 6 hands. Miss it and the run ends.

How to play

Each hand costs a 10-chip ante (20 at Augustus Primack's table). Bet, check, raise or fold on each street, just like Hold'em.

Elite tables seat a mini-boss with a special power. Hover any rival to read their story.

Each table gives you 6 hands to win the target. Miss it and the run ends. A big stack raises the target: it becomes half your chips minus 60 if that beats the table's base.

All in pushes every chip. Most rivals only call with strong hands, but some always call, and losing one ends your run. Multiplier charms don't multiply an all-in pot, only your bonuses.

Between tables buy, sell and reorder charms, and level up hands. Beat all 8 tables to win.

Keys: B bet, C check or call, F fold, R raise, ↑↓ bet size, A all in (press twice), ↵ continue.
